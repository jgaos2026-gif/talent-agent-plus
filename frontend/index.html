Limits — read first

  • Single-process, in-memory v1. Restart clears all data and sessions.
  • Payments are recorded only. Stripe is NOT_CONNECTED. Charges are refused by design.
  • Social posts are drafts, never posted live. Releases export locally, never to a distributor.
  • Cleared money is never toggled by a click. Clearing requires the cleared amount, checked by the engine.
  • This is not a licensed talent agency. Union and CA/NY bookings go through a licensed franchised agent or attorney.
  • This web app is the working app. An APK/AAB for the Play Store is a packaging step, not included here — it needs Android Studio and your Google Play developer account.
